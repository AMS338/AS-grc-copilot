# Client Discovery Checklist

Use this before preparing a proposal. Do not request passwords, personal records, customer information, production banking data, or confidential evidence during initial discovery.

## Organization and objective
- Organization name and industry:
- Country / operating jurisdictions:
- Approximate employee range:
- Main business services:
- Primary reason for seeking GRC support:
- Desired outcome and target date:

## Scope
- Is the priority risk assessment, ISO 27001 readiness, audit evidence, policies, BCM, privacy, or PMO governance?
- Which business units, services, locations, and systems are in scope?
- Is there an existing risk register or control framework?
- Which standards, regulations, customer requirements, or contractual obligations has the client identified?
- Who is the executive sponsor and who owns day-to-day delivery?

## Delivery readiness
- Can the client provide authorized, non-sensitive process descriptions?
- Are control owners available for interviews?
- Does the client have an approved risk methodology?
- Where will client deliverables be stored and who can access them?
- Are there confidentiality, data residency, procurement, or security requirements?

## Qualification
- Is the business problem clear and material?
- Is there an accountable sponsor?
- Is the scope achievable within the proposed timeline?
- Is budget and procurement approval available?
- Can the client accept the stated limitations and responsibilities?

## Next step
Send a written proposal defining:
- Scope and exclusions
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Client inputs and dependencies
- Timeline and meeting cadence
- Fee, currency, payment schedule, and taxes if applicable
- Confidentiality and data-handling arrangements
- Change-control and cancellation terms

Do not begin work until both parties accept the written terms.
