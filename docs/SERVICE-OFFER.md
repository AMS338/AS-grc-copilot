# AS GRC Copilot — Service Offer

## Practical GRC setup for growing businesses

A fixed-scope, plain-language service to help a small organization get a clearer view of its cybersecurity risks and compliance work.

### Starter engagement: GRC Foundation

**Illustrative pilot scope**
- One discovery call (up to 45 minutes)
- One scoped risk register with agreed likelihood and impact definitions
- Up to 10 prioritized risks based on information supplied by the client
- A treatment action plan with suggested owners and target dates
- A short management summary and handover session

**Not included**
- Certification, audit opinion, legal advice, penetration testing, technical remediation, or a guarantee of compliance
- Review of regulated or confidential production data unless a separate, secure engagement and data-handling process is agreed
- Unlimited revisions or work outside the agreed scope

### Optional follow-on services
1. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 readiness and control-applicability workshop
2. Audit evidence register setup
3. Policy and procedure documentation support
4. Risk treatment and management reporting support
5. PMO governance, action tracking, and steering-committee reporting

### Pricing approach
Start with a discovery conversation and confirm the scope, deliverables, timeline, assumptions, and fee in writing before work begins. Any public pilot price is a test price, not a promise of market value or guaranteed demand. Do not start paid work until the client has accepted the written scope and payment terms.

### Client responsibilities
- Provide accurate, authorized, non-sensitive information for the agreed scope.
- Assign accountable business owners to review and approve risk decisions.
- Approve the organization's risk methodology and acceptance criteria.
- Retain ownership of compliance decisions, evidence, and remediation.
