I’m currently opening a small number of discovery conversations to understand where teams are experiencing the most friction and whether a focused GRC foundation engagement would help.

Best,
[Your name]
[Approved business contact]

## Follow-up

Hello [Name],

Just following up on my note. If risk visibility, audit preparation, or ISO 27001 readiness is currently a priority, I’d be happy to have a short conversation and see whether there is a well-defined starting scope.

## Personalization checklist
- Refer to a real business priority the recipient has publicly shared.
- Do not claim knowledge of internal security gaps.
- Do not promise certification or guaranteed compliance.
- Do not send bulk unsolicited messages or misrepresent credentials.
- Use only contact details and claims you are authorized to publish.
